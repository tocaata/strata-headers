# Privacy Policy — Header Editor (Modify Request & Response Headers)

Last updated: 2026-07-10

Header Editor does not collect, store, transmit or sell any user data.

- All rule profiles you create are stored locally in your browser via
  `chrome.storage.local`. They never leave your device.
- The extension makes no network requests of its own and communicates
  with no external server.
- Header modification and URL redirection are performed entirely by
  Chrome's built-in `declarativeNetRequest` API; the extension does not
  read, log or inspect your web traffic.
- No analytics, no telemetry, no cookies.

If you have questions about this policy, please open an issue on the
project repository.
